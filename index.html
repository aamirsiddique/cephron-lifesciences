    Frequently Asked Questions

    Common questions about our PCD pharma franchise, products, and eligibility

    What is the PCD pharma franchise offered by Cephron LifeSciences?

    Cephron LifeSciences offers a monopoly-based PCD (Propaganda Cum Distribution) pharma franchise with exclusive territorial rights, complete marketing support including MR bags, visual aids, and brochures, a wide range of WHO-GMP certified pharmaceutical products, and low initial investment requirements.

    What are the requirements to start a PCD pharma franchise with Cephron LifeSciences?

    To start a PCD pharma franchise you need a valid Drug License, GST Registration, and the investment capacity depending on your selected product range and territory. Basic knowledge of pharma marketing is preferred but not mandatory. Medical representatives, first-time entrepreneurs, and existing distributors are all welcome.

    Is Cephron LifeSciences ISO certified?

    Yes. Cephron LifeSciences is ISO 9001:2015 certified. All products are sourced from WHO-GMP certified manufacturing facilities, adhering to the Drugs & Cosmetics Act and Indian pharmaceutical regulatory standards.

    Where is Cephron LifeSciences located?

    Cephron LifeSciences has its corporate office at Jannat Complex, Kalyanpur, Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh – 226022, and a head office at Aman Park, Shil Phata Road, Thane, Maharashtra – 400612.

    What types of pharmaceutical products does Cephron LifeSciences offer?

    Cephron LifeSciences offers a comprehensive range including tablets, capsules, syrups, injections, ointments, drops, nutraceuticals, herbal products, medical devices, and healthcare consumables across general medicine, pediatrics, chronic care, and specialty segments.
